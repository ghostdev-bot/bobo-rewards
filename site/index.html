How it works (and how it's measured)

1 · Holder rewards coins

On pump.fun a coin can route its creator fee to holders instead of a creator. The fee accrues in a per-coin vault (PDA ["holder-rewards", mint] of the pump program) and pump.fun's distributor (HckQ93…Zg9t) pays it out several times a day with the distribute_fee_to_holders instruction, pro rata to wallets holding at least about $20 of the coin. Payouts are in the coin's quote asset.

2 · BOBO and its pairs

$BOBO trades against SOL, so BOBO holders get SOL. Pairs are coins launched on pump.fun with BOBO as the quote asset (a "custom pair"), so their holders get BOBO: $GRR (Smol Grr) and $STORMIE run a 3% rate. $GRRL is paired to GRR and pays in GRR. 20% of GRR supply sits at the BoboBurn address, so part of GRR's BOBO rewards flows there.

3 · What this site reads

Your wallet's full transaction history since holder rewards launched (Helius archive RPC), keeping only transactions where pump.fun's program runs DistributeFeeToHolders. The coin is the instruction's mint account, and the amount is your wallet's SOL or token balance change in that transaction. Results are cached in your browser, so the next visit only fetches what's new. Balances come from Jupiter (or Helius), prices from DexScreener and Jupiter.

4 · Limits, honestly

USD values use today's price, not the price at payout time. The “above ~$20 minimum” tag is an estimate from your current balance. Very active wallets are read in chunks of 5,000 transactions (“Scan more” continues). If Helius is unavailable the page falls back to free public RPCs, which only keep about the last 1.5 days, and says so.

Data settings

Only used if the built-in Helius connection is unavailable: tried before the free fallback endpoints (publicnode, Solana Tracker). Stored only in this browser.

Unofficial community tool. Not made by, endorsed by, or affiliated with Bobo the Bear, the Bear Council, @bobocouncil, @rekt_tekashi, Smol Grr or pump.fun. Token names and tickers belong to their projects.

Read-only: this site never asks for a signature or sends a transaction. Figures are read from public data and may be incomplete or delayed; anything marked estimate is extrapolated. Informational only.

Data: Helius (Solana archive RPC), with publicnode and Solana Tracker as fallbacks; Jupiter (holdings, prices); DexScreener (prices, market caps, token images from each project's DexScreener profile).
